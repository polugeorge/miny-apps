Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the sleep sounds app published on the App Store under the bundle ID com.miny.sleepy (“the app”) handles, and why. The short version: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

Information stored on your device and in your iCloud

The app stores your progress and settings, such as unlocked items, room decorations, sound preferences, your onboarding answers, bedtime reminder time and language choice. This data is kept on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it is also synced through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage so it can move to your other devices. We do not have access to your iCloud data. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To check which purchases you own and to restore them, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created by the app (not your name or email), your purchase and subscription history for the app, and basic technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, country or region and IP address. We use this only to provide purchases, restore them and understand subscription performance in aggregate.

Notifications

If you turn on bedtime or trial reminders, the app schedules them locally on your device. Nothing about them is sent to us.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don’t use advertising, ad identifiers (IDFA) or tracking across other apps and websites.
  • We don’t sell or share your information for advertising.
  • We don’t collect health data, location, contacts, photos or microphone audio.

Data retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. To remove synced data, delete the app’s data in your iCloud settings. To ask us to delete the purchase records linked to your random identifier at RevenueCat, contact us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
