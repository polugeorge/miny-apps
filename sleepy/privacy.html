migg Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information migg, the sleep sounds app published on the App Store under the bundle ID com.miny.sleepy (“the app”) handles, and why. The short version: the app has no accounts and shows no ads, your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud, and the only usage data we see is anonymous.

Information stored on your device and in your iCloud

The app stores your progress and settings, such as unlocked items, room decorations, sound preferences, your onboarding answers, bedtime reminder time and language choice. This data is kept on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it is also synced through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage so it can move to your other devices. We do not have access to your iCloud data. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To check which purchases you own and to restore them, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created by the app (not your name or email), your purchase and subscription history for the app, and basic technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, country or region and IP address. We use this only to provide purchases, restore them and understand subscription performance in aggregate. RevenueCat also tells Tenjin when a purchase or subscription happens, as described under “Measuring our ads” below.

Notifications

If you turn on bedtime or trial reminders, the app schedules them locally on your device. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback from the app, we receive what you wrote, the kind of feedback you picked, and the app version, OS version, device model and app language. It is collected through Google Forms and is not linked to your name, email or the purchase identifier above. Please don’t include personal information in your message. We use feedback only to fix and improve the app.

Anonymous usage data

To see where the app is confusing and whether its introduction works, the app sends a small number of anonymous events to PostHog: that the app was opened, which introduction screens were shown and for how long, the choices picked there (for example the sound you chose or your usual bedtime range), whether notifications were allowed, and whether the subscription screen was shown or a purchase completed. Each event carries a random identifier created by the app on that install, the app version and app language. It does not include the name you type into the app, anything you write, your purchase identifier, or any advertising identifier, and we do not build a profile from it or combine it with other data. As with any internet request, PostHog’s servers see the IP address the request came from, which is used to derive a country or region. Deleting the app removes the random identifier.

Measuring our ads

We advertise migg on other platforms, and we use Tenjin to see how well those ads work — for example, how many people installed the app after an ad, and whether they went on to subscribe. When the app opens, Tenjin receives a random identifier created on that install, an identifier Apple gives our apps on your device (the vendor identifier, not the advertising identifier), and basic technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, language, country or region and IP address. RevenueCat tells Tenjin when a purchase or subscription happens, so the two can be matched. None of this is linked to your name or email. The app does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) and never asks for permission to track you.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don’t show ads inside the app.
  • We don’t use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and we don’t ask to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • We don’t sell your information.
  • We don’t collect health data, location, contacts, photos or microphone audio.

Data retention and deletion

You can erase your data at any time from inside the app: open the Account tab and choose Delete all data. This removes your room, progress and onboarding answers from your device and from iCloud, and your other devices follow the next time they sync. Deleting the app alone removes only the copy on that device; the iCloud copy stays until you use Delete all data.

Delete all data does not cancel a subscription, which you manage in your Apple account settings, and it does not remove the purchase records held by RevenueCat or the ad measurement records held by Tenjin. To ask us to delete the records linked to your random identifier, contact us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
